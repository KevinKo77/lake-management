Financial Intelligence · LANOVA Finance

Your budget, your cash and your commitments, month by month.

LANOVA Finance turns the budget of a sports organisation into a monthly forecast, compares scenarios and tests investment, financing and staffing decisions against the cash they need. It is in its pilot phase.

01 Planning & forecasting

A monthly plan built from your own accounts.

Import the budget by account and by month from CSV or Excel, or enter it directly. Every line keeps its source, its date and whether it is observed or assumed.

  • 12, 24 or 36 months: revenue, payroll, operating costs, capital spending and loans
  • Income statement and cash forecast that reconcile, with the bridge between them
  • Missing months are flagged, never set to zero
Build a forecast in the demonstration

02 Cash flow & liquidity

The month when cash is tightest.

Month-end cash for each scenario against your minimum cash policy, with the lowest month and the headroom left. A prudent intra-month bound is labelled as a bound, not a forecast.

  • Lowest month-end cash and liquidity headroom
  • Receivables and payables at the end of the horizon
  • Investment capacity that keeps the policy
Analyse cash flow in the demonstration

03 Scenarios & risk

Downside and upside, side by side.

Each scenario has its own assumptions, applied from the month you choose. Risk flags are transparent rules on the calculated figures and your own thresholds, not predictions.

  • Base, downside and upside cases, plus your own
  • Revenue, EBITDA, lowest cash, headroom and debt per scenario
  • Cash policy and debt-service thresholds checked in every case
Compare scenarios in the demonstration

04 Investment decisions

Test the decision against the whole plan.

An investment and its financing, a new loan, a payroll or cost change, a loss of revenue: each one recalculated in every scenario, against the same plan without it.

  • Effect on EBITDA, net result, cash at the end and the lowest month
  • Investment payments, depreciation, revenue and costs it adds
  • Simple EBITDA yield and payback, explicitly not an IRR or an NPV
Explore a decision report

05 Financial commitments

What you owe, against what you generate.

Debt service, transfer instalments, other commitments and capital spending against operating cash flow. Cover ratios are internal indicators, not covenant calculations.

  • Commitments of the first 12 months and those after the horizon
  • Board report as PDF, CSV and HTML
  • Every figure traceable to its source and evidence date
