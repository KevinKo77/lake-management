How to read the probability and interval

The target is actual promotion to Primera the following season, including the playoff winner. Features use only matches completed by the shown date; training and sigmoid calibration use earlier seasons. The interval reflects parameter instability from training-season and calibration-club bootstraps. It is not a guaranteed confidence interval or a guarantee about the match outcome. Marginal club probabilities are not constrained to sum to exactly three promotions.

02 / Financial decision overlay

Fictional EUR assumptions

Promotion uplift begins in model year 2 and persists over the remaining horizon. This is a hypothetical path with no subsequent relegation, tax, financing or timing-of-instalment model. The policy envelope is capped by existing cash; future revenue is not treated as available cash. A common promotion probability applies to every recruitment option, with no causal uplift attributed to a signing.

Data quality, methodology and limitations

Source: openfootball/espana, CC0-1.0. Promotion labels inferred from next-season Primera membership, with explicit club aliases and reserve-team exclusions. Raw inputs, pinned commit, SHA-256 hashes, features, coefficients, backtest predictions and evaluation are versioned with this application. The source is a retrospectively corrected archive, not a verified point-in-time data feed. Small samples, repeated snapshots and competition changes limit generalisation.
