This simulator uses the shared Lake Intelligence cash-flow engine for discounted cash flows. It does not predict sporting results, estimate player performance or provide investment advice. All assumptions are fictional.

Decision assumptions

Transfer fees are assumed paid immediately. Wages are paid annually and escalated at the selected rate. Agent fees are paid initially. Discounting uses annual year-end cash flows. No taxes, financing, accounting amortization or sporting probabilities are included.
