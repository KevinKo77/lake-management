Advanced settings and exact assumptions

These explicit values override the basic club fields and are included in the confirmation preview.

Blank optional player fields use the existing engine defaults: zero agent/signing/expected bonus and attributable revenue; no wage growth; permanent fees paid at signing; loan wage share 100%; exit at contract end; zero resale. Early release pays remaining wages in full; unpaid signing bonus remains payable. These are modelling conventions, not guaranteed prudent assumptions. Enter overrides in the player table.

Fraction fields (wageShare, wageGrowth, sellOnPct, saleCostPct, instalments) use fractions, e.g. 0.5 = 50%. Separate instalments with |. No tax, employer-charge, FX-risk or intra-season cash model.
