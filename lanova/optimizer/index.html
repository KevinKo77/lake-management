Decision evidence, with explicit limits.

Exact within the shortlist

Every subset of up to 16 available candidates is considered. Cost and count limits prune only impossible branches. Results are ranked by your selected objective; ties prefer downside NPV, base NPV, then lower initial spending.

One shared finance engine

Transfer and agent fees at time zero. Annual wages escalate; assumed incremental revenue is constant. Resale arrives in the final year. Net present value uses the existing Lake Intelligence engine and year-end cash flows.

Assumptions remain assumptions

No live scouting feed, player valuation certification or causal sporting model. No tax, financing, instalments, injury or relegation model. Historical promotion research remains a separate, labelled screen.
