How the forecasts are tested before anyone relies on them

A forecast is only worth showing if it has been tested on matches it had not seen. This page reports how LANOVA's engine performs on past LaLiga seasons, replayed one matchday at a time — match results and relegation risks separately, with their uncertainty — and lets you re-run any past season yourself.

Status: validation version 2, after correcting the league ranking rules (head-to-head first). No probability for the current season is published: the current-season data are incomplete, the relegation advantage is not established on unseen seasons, and independent review is pending. LANOVA is independent of LaLiga and of every club named here.
