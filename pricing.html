The detail

Billing, limits and cancellation.

Billing
€990 per month per organisation, excluding VAT, charged in euros in advance each month by card through Stripe. Your billing address and, for businesses, your VAT or tax ID are collected at checkout for the invoice. The subscription renews automatically every month until cancelled.
Cancellation
Cancel at any time from the billing portal in your workspace. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month; access continues until then. Paid months are not refunded pro rata.
Failed payments
If a payment fails, Stripe retries it and we notify the account owner. Access is suspended if the subscription becomes unpaid or is cancelled, and restored when payment succeeds. Saved data is kept.
Users
Up to 5 named users belonging to the subscribing organisation. Accounts may not be shared between organisations.
Data
Your analyses are visible only to your organisation. You can export them at any time. LANOVA does not supply player data or scouting information.
Support
Email support on business days at kevin@lake-am.com.
Nature of the service
LANOVA is decision-support software. Its results depend on the inputs entered by the club and are not financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can we try it before subscribing?

Yes. The live demonstration is free and uses fictional clubs. We also run a 30-minute demo with your own shortlist on request.

Does LANOVA provide player data?

No. LANOVA is a financial decision tool. You enter or import your shortlist and its contract terms; LANOVA calculates the financial consequences.

Does LANOVA predict how a player will perform?

No. Expected league points are the club's own estimates. LANOVA shows how much the recommendation depends on them.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Club Access is billed month to month and can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the paid month.

Who can see our analyses?

Only the users of your organisation. Access to saved data is checked on the server for every request.
