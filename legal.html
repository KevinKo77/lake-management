Nature of the service

LANOVA Sports is decision-support software for team-sport organisations, with football recruitment as its initial supported implementation. It calculates the financial consequences of recruitment options from the inputs entered by each club. Its results are not financial, investment, legal, accounting or tax advice, and do not constitute a recommendation to buy or sell any financial instrument. Lake Management does not manage funds or assets for clients through this website, does not raise capital and holds no regulatory authorisation for investment services. Demonstration clubs and figures shown on this website are fictional.

Privacy

This website. The marketing pages do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the pages load. The demo request form does not send data to any server: it opens your own email application with a pre-filled message.

Live demonstrations. The public demonstrations run entirely in your browser. Inputs are kept only in your browser's local storage and are never uploaded.

Club accounts. For subscribing organisations we process account data (name, work email, organisation, role), the analyses and scenarios saved in the workspace, and subscription status. This data is used only to provide the service, is visible only to users of the same organisation, and is not sold or shared with other clubs. Payments are processed by Stripe, which acts as an independent payment processor; LANOVA never receives or stores card numbers.

Your rights. Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of your personal data, and object to its processing, by writing to kevin@lake-am.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).

Retention. Account and workspace data are kept for the duration of the subscription and deleted or returned on request after it ends, except where the law requires billing records to be kept.

Subscription terms (summary)

Organisation Access costs €990 per month, billed monthly in advance through Stripe, and renews automatically until cancelled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month. Full terms are provided before subscription. See billing, limits and cancellation.
