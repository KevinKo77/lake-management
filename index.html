01 Every resource is a decision

Turn money into results without putting the organisation at risk.

Every sports organisation, whatever its size or sport, faces the same question: where should the next euro go? A coach, a facility, an academy, a signing, a commercial project — or the reserve. The answer depends on cost, timing, cash and what the organisation is trying to achieve.

A

Every euro has an alternative use

Options compete for the same budget. LANOVA puts them side by side on their full cost, including what they commit you to after this season.

B

Now, later, or safe

Immediate performance, long-term development and financial security pull in different directions. LANOVA makes the trade-off explicit instead of hiding it in a single score.

C

Cash timing decides what is possible

An option can fit the annual budget and still break the minimum cash reserve in one month. LANOVA checks every month before anything is ranked.

D

Unknowns stay visible

Estimates are labelled as estimates. A missing figure is shown as missing, never silently counted as zero, and the decision brief lists what is still needed.

02 The Decision Engine

Compare options under real constraints.

The Advantage Lab ranks nothing until every hard constraint is met.

  • Full cost of each option: immediate cost, running costs, commitments after the horizon
  • Hard constraints first: the budget available and the minimum cash reserve, month by month
  • Infeasible and dominated options identified, with the reason
  • Three strategies — immediate benefits, long-term development, financial security — over every feasible combination
  • A decision brief with assumptions, limits and missing data, as HTML, JSON or CSV

05 From information to a decision

Six steps, one explainable record.

Each step is visible in the tools, and the decision brief records it for the board.

  1. Resources

    Cash today, revenue, costs and the budget you can commit.

  2. Objectives

    Immediate performance, development or financial return.

  3. Scenarios

    Base and downside cases, applied to every option.

  4. Constraints

    Budget and minimum cash reserve, checked month by month.

  5. Trade-offs

    Dominated, infeasible and efficient options; three strategies.

  6. Report

    A decision brief with assumptions, limits and missing data.
