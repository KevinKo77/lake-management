What LANOVA answers

The financial questions behind every decision.

01 · Planning & forecasting

Where will the budget land?

A monthly budget over 12, 24 or 36 months, imported from your accounts, turned into an income statement and a cash forecast that reconcile.

02 · Cash flow & liquidity

When is cash tightest?

Month-end cash for every scenario against your minimum cash policy, with a prudent intra-month bound and the month where headroom is lowest.

03 · Scenarios & risk

What if revenue falls?

Base, downside and upside cases with their own assumptions, compared side by side, and transparent risk flags on the calculated figures.

04 · Investment decisions

Can we afford it, and how?

An investment, a loan, a payroll or cost change, a signing: each one recalculated in every scenario, with its effect on cash, results and debt service.

05 · Financial commitments

Can we meet what we owe?

Debt service, transfer instalments and other commitments set against the cash the operations generate, with a commitments cover indicator.

06 · Squad economics

What does a squad decision commit us to?

A specialised module for recruitment: the multi-season cost of each signing and the best combination within the budget, squad-cost and cash limits.
