The questions behind every signing

A transfer fee is only the first number.

Wages rise, instalments fall due, bonuses and agent fees add up, and the squad-cost limit applies every season, not just this window. LANOVA puts the whole commitment in front of the people who approve it.

01

What does this signing really cost?

Fee, instalments, agent fee, signing bonus, wages and expected bonuses across the full contract.

02

Can we afford it in every season?

Budget at signing, squad cost per season and the lowest cash point, checked against your limits.

03

Which combination is best?

The best set of signings for your objective that respects every limit, not just the best single player.

04

What if things go badly?

A bad case with lower resale and revenue shows how low cash can fall and what the club is exposed to.

LANOVA does not guess which player will succeed. It shows, before you commit, exactly what each decision costs, which limits it uses up and what happens to the club's cash if the season goes badly.
