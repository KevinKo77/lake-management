Who asks for a demo

Finance directors, executives, owners and investors

Finance directors and chief executives of clubs, federations and leagues; owners and boards who need a decision they can check; investors and M&A advisers who want to understand the cash and commitments of an organisation. LANOVA does not value clubs or companies.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. Send the email: your organisation, your role, your sport, the decision you want to look at and a few times that suit you.
  2. We reply by email to fix a 30-minute call.
  3. In the call we work on that decision, with an illustrative example or the figures you choose to share.

Please do not send confidential figures by email. Terms of Organisation Access and of the finance pilot: see Access & pricing.
