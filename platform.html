04 Principles

Calculated is not estimated.

Every screen keeps the difference between what LANOVA calculates and what your organisation estimates.

1

Calculated

Costs, commitments, cash month by month and feasibility come from the engines and your figures.

2

Estimated

Contributions to sporting performance or development are your estimates, labelled as such. LANOVA does not predict sporting results and shows no probability.

3

Unknown

A missing estimate stays missing. It is neither counted as zero nor guessed; a ranking that depends on it is reported as uncertain.

4

Constrained first

Budget and minimum cash are hard constraints, checked before any preference or score.
