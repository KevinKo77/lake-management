Platform

The financial side of recruitment, in one workspace.

Everything on this page is implemented and can be tried in the live demonstration with fictional clubs. The screenshots are taken from the application itself.

Open the live demonstrationRequest a Demo

1 · Recruitment financial analysis

Every option, priced line by line.

For each candidate, LANOVA models the deal as the club would actually pay it: permanent transfer, free transfer or loan.

  • Transfer or loan fee, paid in instalments
  • Agent fee and signing bonus
  • Wages with annual growth, and expected bonuses
  • Resale value, sell-on clauses, sale costs and early-exit obligations
  • Cash per season and accounting cost per season

2 · Total financial commitment

The whole contract, not just the fee.

The dashboard adds up what the club commits to across every season of every contract: paid at signing, added squad cost per season and total contract commitments, set against the budget and the squad-cost limit.

  • Budget at signing and what is left
  • Added squad cost in each season of the plan
  • Total commitments across all contracts

3 · Scenario comparison

Compare decisions before you make them.

Run the analysis, change a limit, a player's terms or a choice in the shortlist, and run it again. LANOVA compares the two scenarios side by side and lists exactly which inputs changed.

  • Recommended signings in each scenario
  • Payment at signing, total commitments, added squad cost
  • Lowest cash in the bad case

4 · Budget optimisation

The best combination within every limit.

LANOVA searches the combinations of your shortlist and returns the best one for your objective: financial value, league points you expect, or a blend of both. A combination is only eligible if it respects every limit you set. The search is exact: no feasible combination is skipped.

  • Transfer budget, squad-cost limit per season, cash floor
  • Squad size, non-EU places, maximum number of signings
  • Positions to fill, players you must sign or exclude
  • Binding limits and headroom shown for every constraint

5 · Risk and downside analysis

Know the bad case before it happens.

Every analysis includes a bad case in which every season goes badly: lower resale values and lower player revenue. LANOVA shows the lowest point of the club's cash, the present value of the downside, and whether the recommendation holds when assumptions move.

  • Lowest cash in the bad case, against your safety level
  • Downside loss in present value, with an optional cap
  • Stress tests on points, resale, discount rate and point value
  • What would have to change for the answer to change

6 · Decision reporting and exports

A record the board can read.

Every analysis can leave the platform as a document your board, owner or auditor can check. Exports are only available for results that match the current inputs, so an outdated result is never presented as current.

  • Decision report: recommendation, constraints, reasons, risks and alternatives
  • Payment and cost schedules as CSV, season by season
  • Scenario inputs as JSON, to reload or archive

Boundaries

What the platform does not do.

LANOVA is a financial decision tool. It does not provide player data, scouting reports or predictions of sporting performance. Expected points and resale values are the club's own estimates, and the platform shows how much the decision depends on them.
