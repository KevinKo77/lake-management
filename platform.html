Platform

One financial platform for sports institutions.

LANOVA Finance plans the budget, forecasts cash, compares scenarios and tests investment, financing and squad decisions against your financial commitments. Squad Economics is a specialised module for squad decisions. The screenshots on this page are taken from the applications themselves, with illustrative anonymous examples.

Explore the demonstrationsRequest a demo

Availability: LANOVA Finance is in its pilot phase (on request, not included in Organisation Access). Squad Economics is available today in Organisation Access.

1 · Financial Planning & Forecasting · LANOVA Finance (pilot)

A monthly plan built from your own accounts.

Import the budget from CSV or Excel, by account and by month, or enter it directly. Each line keeps its source, its date and whether it is observed or assumed.

  • 12, 24 or 36 months; revenue, payroll, operating costs, capital spending and loans
  • Income statement and cash forecast that reconcile, with the bridge between them
  • Collection and payment delays per line; missing months are flagged, never set to zero

2 · Cash Flow & Liquidity Analysis · LANOVA Finance (pilot)

The month when cash is tightest.

Month-end cash for each scenario against your minimum cash policy, with the lowest month and the headroom left. A prudent intra-month bound shows what happens if every payment of a month came before its receipts — clearly labelled as a bound, not a forecast.

  • Lowest month-end cash and liquidity headroom against your policy
  • Receivables and payables carried at the end of the horizon
  • Investment capacity that keeps the policy in every month

3 · Scenario & Risk Analysis · LANOVA Finance (pilot)

Downside and upside, side by side.

Each scenario has its own assumptions — revenue and cost changes by category, later collections, higher rates on new loans — applied from the month you choose. Risk flags are transparent rules on the calculated figures and your own thresholds, not predictions.

  • Base, downside and upside cases, plus your own
  • Revenue, EBITDA, net result, lowest cash, headroom and debt per scenario
  • Cash policy, payroll-to-revenue and debt-service thresholds checked in every case

4 · Investment Decision Support · LANOVA Finance (pilot)

Test the decision against the whole plan.

An investment and its financing, a new loan, a payroll or cost change, a loss of revenue, a signing: each decision is recalculated in every scenario, alone and combined with the others, against the same plan without it.

  • Effect on EBITDA, net result, cash at the end and the lowest month
  • Investment payments, depreciation, operating revenue and costs it adds
  • Simple EBITDA yield and payback — explicitly not an IRR or an NPV

5 · Financial Commitments · LANOVA Finance (pilot)

What the organisation owes, against what it generates.

Loan debt service, transfer instalments of past and new deals, other commitments and capital spending, set against operating cash flow. The commitments cover and the debt-service cover are internal indicators, labelled as such — not covenant calculations.

  • Commitments of the first 12 months and those falling after the horizon
  • Board report as PDF, CSV and HTML; saved revisions with their history in the pilot application
  • Every figure traceable to its source and evidence date

Boundaries

What the platform does not do.

LANOVA is decision-support software. It calculates the consequences of the figures and assumptions your organisation enters; it is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. It does not value clubs or companies, compute tax, certify covenants, supply player data or scouting reports, or predict sporting results. Scenario results are conditional on their assumptions; no probability is attached to them.
