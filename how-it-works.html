How It Works

From the budget to a decision the board can sign off.

With LANOVA Finance: six steps from your accounts to a board report. The same discipline applies to squad decisions in the Squad Economics module.

Import the budget

Monthly budget by account from CSV or Excel, mapped to LANOVA categories. Every line keeps its source and evidence date; ambiguous amounts, months outside the plan and missing months are refused or flagged, never guessed.

Set the financial position

Opening cash, the minimum cash the organisation wants to keep, existing loans, transfer instalments, investments already committed, and the receivables and payables at the start of the plan.

Read the forecast

Monthly income statement and cash forecast over 12, 24 or 36 months, with the bridge from result to cash, the lowest month and the financial commitments of the first year.

Compare scenarios

Base, downside and upside cases with their own assumptions, from the month you choose. Only the scenario you change moves.

Test decisions

An investment and its financing, a new loan, a payroll or cost plan, a loss of revenue, a signing: each one recalculated against the same plan without it, in every scenario.

Report and keep the record

Export the board report as PDF, CSV or HTML. In the pilot application, each saved plan keeps its revisions, so the figures behind a decision can be found again.

Squad Economics · available today

For squad decisions: from a shortlist to a recommendation.

Set the transfer budget, squad-cost and cash limits; enter or import the shortlist with each candidate's contract terms; confirm the inputs; LANOVA returns the best combination within every limit, with the binding constraints, the downside and the alternatives; compare and export the decision report and CSV schedules.

Your data

Built for confidential decisions.

Private workspace

Each organisation has its own workspace. Saved plans and analyses are visible only to that organisation's users, and access is checked on the server for every request.

Your figures stay yours

LANOVA uses only the figures your organisation enters. They are never shared with other organisations or used to build a database. The public demonstrations run in your browser and send nothing.

Payments by Stripe

Subscriptions are billed by Stripe. LANOVA never sees or stores card numbers.

Getting started

Onboarding

Organisation Access subscribers receive their workspace and a 60-minute onboarding session to set up their limits and first shortlist. For the LANOVA Finance pilot, the data to prepare, the reconciliation of totals and the onboarding are agreed with the organisation before any figure is shared.
