How It Works

From resources to a decision the board can sign off.

The same six steps in every sport. The Advantage Lab compares options; LANOVA Finance gives the monthly cash behind them; the decision brief records the reasoning.

  1. Resources

    Cash today, annual revenue and costs, the budget available for these decisions, the minimum cash reserve.

  2. Objectives

    Immediate performance, long-term development or financial return.

  3. Scenarios

    A base case and a downside case with lower revenue, applied to every option.

  4. Constraints

    Budget and reserve are checked every month; options that break them are set aside with the reason.

  5. Trade-offs

    Dominated options are identified; three strategies compare every feasible combination.

  6. Report

    The decision brief: objective, costs, constraints, alternatives, assumptions, limits, missing data.

In the Advantage Lab

Options, compared honestly.

  • Enter each option: immediate cost, running cost and how long it commits you, any financial gain you can estimate
  • Give your own 0–10 estimates of its contribution and your view of its risk — or leave them blank: unknown stays unknown
  • Read the map: efficient, dominated, not feasible, not comparable
  • Compare the three strategies and export the brief
