How It Works

From a shortlist to a decision the board can sign off.

Six steps, usually completed in one working session once the club's limits are known.

Set the club's limits

Transfer budget, squad-cost limit for each season of the plan, wages already committed, cash today and the minimum cash to keep, squad and non-EU places, the maximum number of signings and the positions to fill.

Build the shortlist

Enter candidates or import them from CSV or Excel. For each one: deal type, fee and instalments, agent fee, signing bonus, wages and growth, contract length, expected bonuses, resale assumptions and the league points you expect from the player. These are your estimates; LANOVA does not invent them.

Confirm the inputs

Before any calculation, LANOVA shows the values it will use, converted and normalised, and asks you to confirm them. Invalid or missing values are flagged instead of being guessed.

Run the analysis

LANOVA evaluates the combinations of your shortlist and returns the best one for your objective within every limit, with the reasons, the binding constraints, the risks and the next-best alternatives.

Test and compare

Change a limit, a contract term or a choice and run again. The new result is compared with the previous one, and stress tests show whether the recommendation holds when points, resale values or the discount rate move.

Export the decision

Download the decision report and the payment and cost schedules. Exports are only possible for results that match the current inputs.

Your data

Built for confidential decisions.

Private workspace

Each subscribing organisation has its own workspace. Saved analyses and scenarios are visible only to that organisation's users, and access is checked on the server for every request.

Your inputs stay yours

LANOVA uses only the figures your club enters. They are never shared with other clubs or used to build a database.

Payments by Stripe

Subscriptions are billed by Stripe. LANOVA never sees or stores card numbers.

Getting started

Onboarding

After subscribing, the club receives access to its workspace and a 60-minute onboarding session to set up its limits and first shortlist.
